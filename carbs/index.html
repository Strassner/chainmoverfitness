The best carbs for fat loss

Carbs were never the enemy. The right ones keep you full, steady your blood sugar, and let your metabolism work with you instead of against you. Start here.

  1. 1
    White potatoes

    The most filling food per calorie there is. Nothing keeps you full like a potato.

  2. 2
    Sweet potatoes

    Filling, a little sweet, and loaded with the nutrients your body runs on.

  3. 3
    Oats

    Steady energy, and easy to prep ahead of a busy week.

  4. 4
    Berries

    Blueberries, strawberries, raspberries. High fiber, low sugar, top of the fruit list.

  5. 5
    Apples

    Fiber and water. One of the most filling fruits there is.

  6. 6
    Orange juice

    Fights inflammation, feeds your metabolism, and gives you quick energy when you need it. One caveat: if you're extremely insulin resistant, skip it for now.
