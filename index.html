Questions

Straight answers.

What if it is not right for me?
Two things. Your first week is on me: get accepted today and your program clock starts the following Monday, so you get the app, your intake and your first week before you are paying for any of it. And you have seven days from your start date to change your mind, message me directly and I refund you in full, processed within 5 business days. After day seven, refunds are at my discretion.
Do I have to cut carbs?
No, and that is the point. The goal is to have you eating more carbs than you are eating right now and still losing weight. Being afraid of rice or bread for the rest of your life is not a plan.
Do I have to count calories forever?
No. You will track for the first month or two, because that is the fastest way to understand what is actually in your food. After that we move you to eating intuitively.
What if my bloodwork came back normal?
That is common, and it is exactly why this gets missed. Fasting glucose and A1c are lagging indicators, so they only move once insulin can no longer compensate. Reduced energy, afternoon crashes and easier fat gain can all appear years before a lab value shifts.
Can you reverse my insulin resistance or pre-diabetes?
I am a coach, not a doctor, so I will not promise that and you should be careful with anyone who does. What I do is coach the daily behaviors, food, training, sleep, stress and steps, that support better metabolic health. Clients have seen real changes in their markers. You keep your physician in the loop the whole way.
What if I am on medication, or on a GLP-1?
Plenty of clients start on blood pressure medication, metformin or a GLP-1. Nothing here asks you to stop or change a prescription, that is between you and your doctor. Tell me on your application what you are taking and I will build around it.
I am too busy for this.
If a plan only works during the calm weeks of your life, it does not work. Your plan gets built for your worst week, not your best one, because those are the weeks that decide whether this holds.
Do I need a gym?
No. Home setup, full gym or nothing at all, the training gets built around the equipment you actually have.
Do I work with you, or a sub coach?
Me. Capacity stays at 45 clients on purpose so every check in and every adjustment comes from me directly.
What happens after I apply?
I read every application myself. If it looks like a fit, you hear back and we take it from there. Capacity is capped at 45 clients, so not every application gets accepted, and I would rather tell you that up front than waste your time.
